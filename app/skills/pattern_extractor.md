Skill: reusable pattern extractor

Goal:
- Convert observed notes into repeatable content patterns.
- Extract what can be reused without copying the original note.

Rules:
- Look for title formulas, opening hooks, scene setups, proof formats, and comment triggers.
- Prefer patterns that can be applied to multiple future notes.
- Distinguish "copyable structure" from "must-change material".
- Use note details when available; avoid patterns based only on like counts.

Output style:
- Practical templates, not abstract advice.
