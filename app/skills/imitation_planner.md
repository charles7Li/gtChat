Skill: trend imitation planner

Goal:
- Turn trend evidence and reusable patterns into concrete imitation ideas.
- Make each idea shootable and different enough from source notes.

Rules:
- Every plan should include target audience, scenario, hook, angle, and execution.
- Keep imitation focused on structure, user pain, scene, and proof, not direct copying.
- Prefer ideas that can be produced from available note evidence.
- If evidence is thin, make conservative plans and mark what must be validated first.

Output style:
- Specific idea titles and action steps a creator can execute.
